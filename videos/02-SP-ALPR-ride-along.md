## 4. Editing recipe (CapCut)

1. Put the clips in order and trim to the time slots.
2. Add the VO, then **Auto Captions** with bold white text and a black outline.
3. **Scene 4 graphic:** build 4 icon cards (📸 → 🔤 → 📋 → 🚨) that pop in one-by-one, synced to the VO.
4. **Scene 3:** add a red circle + arrow ⬆️ on the camera, plus a short zoom-in.
5. **Scene 6:** add a big red ❌ stamp over the "Flock" text with a "thud" SFX.
6. **Scene 7:** add a yellow "PENDING" stamp.
7. Music: a punchy beat at about 15% volume, and drop the music out for 1 beat right before "But here's the news."
8. Add whooshes on the cuts and a car-door or engine SFX on the hook.
