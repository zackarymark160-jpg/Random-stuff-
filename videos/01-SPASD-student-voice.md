# 🎬 SPASD Student Voice — "Who Decides?" (AI Video Kit)

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 · ~50 sec · TikTok / Reels
**Vibe:** Hype + punchy: quick cuts, bold captions, upbeat trending-style beat
**Narrator:** AI teen student (fictional, NOT a real student) talking to the camera while walking
**Mission:** SPASD Student Voice gets students heard by the School Board and district leaders, because decisions about students should include students.

---

## 1. Character sheet (paste into EVERY prompt for consistency)

> **NARRATOR:** a 16-year-old high school student, friendly confident smile, casual hoodie in orange and black, backpack on one shoulder, natural lighting, realistic, handheld vlog-style camera at chest height, Midwest suburban setting in autumn.

**Tip:** In Runway, Kling, or Veo, generate ONE reference image of this character first, then use it as the "character reference" or "first frame" for every clip so the face stays the same.

## 3. AI video prompts (copy and paste)

Use **Veo 3 / Sora / Kling / Runway Gen-4**. Settings: 9:16, 5–8s per clip, realistic.

1. **Hook:** `[CHARACTER] pushes open a classroom door and walks directly toward the camera, speaking to camera with a curious expression, handheld vlog shot, school hallway behind, bright natural light, vertical 9:16, photorealistic`
2. **Hallway:** `[CHARACTER] walks down a busy high school hallway with lockers, talking to camera, other students passing blurred in background, tracking shot, vertical, photorealistic`
3. **Classroom:** `[CHARACTER] sits at a desk in a bright modern classroom, turns to camera and talks, classmates brainstorming at a whiteboard behind with sticky notes, warm light, vertical, photorealistic`
4. **Exit/transition:** `[CHARACTER] walks out the front doors of a brick suburban high school, autumn trees, whip-pan motion blur at end, vertical`
5. **District Center:** `[CHARACTER] walks into the lobby of a modern school district administration building, glass doors, points at a wall sign, talks to camera, vertical, photorealistic`
6. **Board meeting:** `A teenage student speaks at a podium with a microphone during a public school board meeting, board members seated at a long curved table with nameplates listening, audience chairs, realistic civic meeting room, vertical`
7. **Walk & talk:** `[CHARACTER] walks toward camera on a sidewalk outside a school at golden hour, passionate expression, talking, vertical, photorealistic`
8. **CTA:** `[CHARACTER] stops, points at camera, smiles confidently, close-up, golden hour, vertical, photorealistic`

**Voice:** Use HeyGen or ElevenLabs with a teen-sounding, upbeat voice, or lip-sync with Veo 3's built-in audio. For the most authentic version, have a real student record the VO on their phone.

---

## 4. Editing recipe (CapCut is easiest)

1. Drop clips 1–8 in order and trim each to its time slot.
2. Add the VO, then turn on **Auto Captions** with bold white text and a black outline in the center-lower third.
3. Add the on-screen text from the table as pop-up stickers in the **top third**.
4. Music: an upbeat trending sound at 15–20% volume under the VO.
5. Add a **whoosh SFX** on every cut and a **bass hit** on "Two student seats."
6. Make the first frame the hook text "WHO DECIDES? 👀" so it doubles as the thumbnail.

## ⚠️ Before posting

- **Label it as AI-generated.** TikTok and Instagram require it, and it builds trust.
- **Don't use AI to fake real people.** No real board members, real students, or the real superintendent. Keep everyone fictional.
- **Confirm the facts:** the 7 elected members + 2 non-voting student members are per the [SPASD Board of Education page](https://www.sunprairieschools.org/district/board-of-education). Double-check it before posting.
- **Check with the district** if this goes out under an official SPASD or Student Voice account. They may have branding or approval rules.
