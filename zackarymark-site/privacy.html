Privacy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What this site collects

Only what you type into a form: contact messages, petitions, sign-ups, questions, comments, guestbook notes and RSVPs. Forms are handled by Netlify, the site's host.

How it's used

I read submissions myself. Petition names are never posted — I only share totals. Comments, guestbook notes and questions only appear on the site after I approve them, and only with your first name. I don't sell or share your information.

No tracking

There are no ads, analytics or tracking cookies. Your browser remembers your language, dark mode, text size and game high score on your own device only.

Students

I never post students' names. If you're under 13, please ask a parent before filling out a form.

Remove your info

Email zackarymark160@gmail.com and I'll delete it.
