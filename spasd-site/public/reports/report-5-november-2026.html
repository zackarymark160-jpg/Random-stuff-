Student Voice Report #5

What changed in November

Since Report #4 in October, the student council plan grew to every middle and high school, the Board explained how its student reps are chosen, and weighted grades reached the School Board.

November 9, 2026 · Written by students, for students · 3-minute read

Read online: spasdstudentvoice.netlify.app/reports/report-5-november-2026.html (it may be blocked on school Wi-Fi, so try your phone's data).

What we asked for

Every badge has a word, not just a color: Won, In Progress, Waiting on District, or Not Yet.

  • Won

    District newsletters for students

    We asked: send district newsletters to students, not only to families.

    The district agreed in writing on October 21. Students start getting them in November.

  • Won

    A student update at Board meetings

    We asked: a short student update at every monthly Board meeting.

    The Board President said this already exists. The student representatives get a spot each month.

  • In Progress

    Weighted grades

    We asked: count AP, capstone, and college-credit classes on a 5.0 scale.

    On the Board's November 9 agenda. The goal is a vote on November 23, but that is not guaranteed. It would start with students entering 9th grade in fall 2027.

  • In Progress

    A district-wide student council

    We asked: 3 to 5 students from every middle and high school meeting regularly with district leaders.

    The superintendent met with us on November 4. Invitations went to six schools. One middle school is in and another school said it supports the idea. It is not formally approved yet.

  • Waiting on District

    A bigger student survey

    We asked: share a student survey through district channels, then post a "you said, we did" update.

    Asked for a follow-up meeting with district leaders on November 6. No date yet.

  • Not Yet

    Open this site at school

    We asked: let students open this site on school Chromebooks and Wi-Fi.

    On November 6, district staff confirmed the school filter blocks it. Use your phone's data or this printed copy for now.

What's next

  1. Mon, Nov 23School Board meeting, 6 PM. The goal is a vote on weighted grades. Anyone can sign up to speak for 2 minutes.
  2. DecemberThe Board starts recruiting new junior student representatives.
  3. Mid-JanuaryApplications open for current sophomores who want to be a Board student representative.
  4. Before spring breakA Board committee interviews applicants and picks the new student representatives.

Ask the District: top 2 questions

Students asked. The district answered. Answers are word for word.

  1. When is the final Board vote on weighted grades?

    "No - this will be determined by the questions that come from the TLE committee (next Monday) and the Committee of the Whole Board meeting on Nov 9th. If there are questions or it becomes clear that this model/thinking would not be supported - we'll need to reconvene and reconsider, meaning that there may be additional meetings and work prior to approval. Ideally, this recommendation would be up for formal approval at the Board Meeting on Nov 23rd - but that is not guaranteed at this time."

    Dr. Curtis Mould, Director of Digital Media, Innovation and Strategy · cdmould@sunprairieschools.org · Oct 30, 2026

  2. How are the School Board's student representatives chosen?

    "Student representatives actually serve a two-year term, taking office at the end of their sophomore year and serving through the end of their senior year. The recruitment process for new junior representatives usually gets underway in December, and applications generally open in mid-January for current sophomores. The School Board Member Selection Committee will interview candidates and vote on a candidate for each open seat, typically prior to Spring Break."

    Karen Richmond, Executive Assistant for the Superintendent and Board Leadership · krrichm@sunprairieschools.org · Nov 5, 2026
