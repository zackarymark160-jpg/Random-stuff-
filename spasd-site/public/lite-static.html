Lite version · updated November 10, 2026 · student-run, not an official district site

What's happening

  • Weighted grades: with the School Board. The goal is a vote on November 23 (not guaranteed). It would start with students entering 9th grade in fall 2027.
  • District-wide student council: invitations went to every middle and high school on November 4. One middle school has joined. Not formally approved yet.
  • Newsletters: the district agreed to send its newsletters to students, starting in November.
  • Board student reps: two-year terms. Recruiting starts in December, and applications open in mid-January for current sophomores.

Upcoming dates

  • Mon, Nov 23, 6 PM: School Board meeting, Professional Learning Center, 220 Kroncke Drive. Anyone can sign up to speak for 2 minutes.
  • Mon, Dec 14: School Board meeting.
  • Mid-January: Board student rep applications open for sophomores.
